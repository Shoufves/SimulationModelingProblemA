Yearly Electricity Data

Ember’s latest yearly data on electricity generation, capacity, emissions and demand from over 200 geographies.

Updated monthly

Yearly electricity data – Global (CSV)
Yearly electricity data – Europe only (CSV)

Anchor point: Summary

Summary

This dataset contains yearly electricity generation, capacity, emissions, import and demand data for over 200 geographies. Data is collected from multi-country datasets (EIA, Eurostat, BP, UN) as well as national sources (e.g China data from the National Bureau of Statistics).

Our yearly electricity data is kept up to date throughout the year and is regularly amended with the latest available data. The data is updated twice a month with an update in the first week of the month followed by a second update in the third week of the month.

Note: monthly electricity data can be found here.

 

Data download format update (July 2026)

One row per area, month and electricity source. The “Electricity source” column covers fuels, aggregates, demand and net imports; The column “Is aggregated source” flags aggregates (Clean, Fossil, Renewables, Total generation).

Metrics are columns, not rows. Generation (TWh), Share of generation (%), Emissions (MtCO2e), Share of emissions (%) and Emissions intensity (gCO2e/kWh), Capacity (GW), as well as  year-on-year changes. Emissions intensity is now available per source, not just overall. Total emissions and overall intensity are on the “Total generation” row.

Some labels have changed. Area names now follow simplified names (e.g. United States, Türkiye); membership columns are “EU member” and “OECD member” etc. with TRUE/FALSE instead of 1/0; source names use sentence case.
